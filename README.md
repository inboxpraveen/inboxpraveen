# Praveen Kumar

I'm a Principal AI Engineer at TrellisSoft in Bengaluru. I build speech, document and LLM systems, from the data and models to the application people use.

A lot of my work is in the details around a model: getting a job to recover after a crash, checking an answer against its source, or finding out why a model that fits in memory is still too slow. I share the tools and experiments here, along with what worked and what didn't.

## More of the work

- **Speech and audio:** [Speech Annotation Tool](https://github.com/inboxpraveen/Speech-Annotation-Tool) for reviewing transcripts and preparing datasets; [AudioFP](https://github.com/inboxpraveen/Audio-Fingerprint) for finding clips and repeated audio in recordings.
- **Documents and retrieval:** [contextsearch](https://github.com/inboxpraveen/Context-Search-Engine) combines keyword and embedding search, with the source passage behind each result. [Docuvera360](https://inboxpraveen.github.io/projects/project-2.html) is a case study from my professional work; its code is closed source.
- **Models and evaluation:** [Kavach PII](https://inboxpraveen.github.io/blogs/blog-6.html) covers training a small multilingual model, auditing its data, and measuring where it still fails.
- **Recommendations and learning:** [Movie Recommendation System](https://github.com/inboxpraveen/Movie-Recommendation-System) and [ML Algorithms from Scratch](https://github.com/inboxpraveen/ML-Algorithms-from-scratch).
- **Everyday tools:** [HabitFlow](https://github.com/inboxpraveen/Daily-Tracker), a local habit tracker, and [coding-agent rules](https://github.com/inboxpraveen/Minimize-Cursor-Cost) for reducing unnecessary context and edits.

The [project directory](https://inboxpraveen.github.io/all-projects.html) has the longer write-ups and earlier work. Reuse terms are in each project's license.
